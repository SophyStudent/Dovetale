A disciplined 4-stage engineering sprint methodology

Zero ambiguity. From initial 42-point diagnostic audit to complete infrastructure deployment in 14 to 30 days.

How does it work?

Sprint Week 01

Stage 01: Audit Dossier

Comprehensive 42-point inspection covering client-side tags, ad account match quality, consent banner setups, and webhook failures. You receive a prioritized vulnerability matrix.

Timeline: Days 1 – 5 Lead: Principal Architect
Sprint Week 02

Stage 02: Architecture Spec

Creation of the Technical Architecture Document (TAD). We document every custom event, parameters, data transformations, and edge proxy routing rules before writing code.

Timeline: Days 6 – 10 Lead: Data Systems Lead
Sprint Week 03

Stage 03: Deployment & QA

Provisioning container instances on GCP Cloud Run or AWS Fargate. Deploying server-side tags, bi-directional webhooks, and running automated regression suites.

Timeline: Days 11 – 20 Lead: Infrastructure Engineer
Sprint Week 04

Stage 04: Production Cutover & SLA

Verifying Meta Event Match Quality (EMQ > 8.5) and Google Enhanced Conversions validation. Activating 24/7 automated monitoring agents and 4-hour hotfix SLA.

Timeline: Days 21 – 30 Lead: Technical Governance
