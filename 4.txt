2. Business challenges section
This section comes immediately after the hero. Its purpose is to make visitors recognise problems they may already be experiencing in their own businesses.
White background
Your Marketing Efforts Shouldn't End in Guesswork.
You may be investing in marketing, attracting attention and receiving enquiries, but are the systems behind those activities helping you turn that effort into actual business results?
Your ads generate clicks, but where are the customers?

You spend on Google or Meta ads without clear visibility into what happens after someone clicks.


People visit your website but don't take action.

Visitors browse your pages, abandon forms or leave before completing a purchase.


Enquiries come in, but follow-up isn't consistent.

Messages from WhatsApp, Instagram, email and forms are difficult to manage and track.


You have data, but can't see the full picture.

Your marketing platforms show separate numbers without a clear view of what is working.


Customers buy once, but there's no clear retention process.

You have limited systems for customer follow-up, repeat purchases and long-term relationships.



These aren't isolated marketing problems.
They can be signs that the systems connecting your marketing, website, sales processes and customer data need attention.

Visual direction
- White background with dark navy headings.
- Five clean problem cards with simple teal icons.
- Two-column layout on desktop, one-column layout on mobile.
- Generous spacing; no unnecessary decorative graphics.
One important point: The wording should make visitors recognise their own situation, not simply tell them that their business needs better systems.
